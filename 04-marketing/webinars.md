# Webinar Program — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Astra (Marketing) · Monthly · Target: webinar→MQL ≥25%, regs ≥100/mo.

## 1. Position

Webinars are our highest-intent conversion surface: they prove the research
engine live, put the human owner in front of buyers, and feed the sales queue.
Format = **research reveal + live Q&A**. No product pitches as the main event;
evidence-first, always.

## 3. Format (60 min)

| Segment | Time | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | 5 min | Problem + what's decided today; one claim with source |
| Research reveal | 30 min | 5–6 data points, charts, methodology transparently shown |
| Application | 10 min | What it means for an SMB/mid-market decision-maker (3 takeaways) |
| Live Q&A | 10 min | Owner + Sage answer; no canned answers |
| Close + CTA | 5 min | Single CTA; recording + slide deck offered |

Staffing: **Astra** = logistics/promo/moderation · **Sage** = data prep + live
stats · **Owner** = presenter (human accountability) · **Vector** = sales follow-up
queue · **Euclid** = tech, metrics.

## 5. Landing page

```
H1:  [Title] — a Meridian research reveal + live Q&A
Sub: Date/time (include timezone), 60 min
What you'll learn: 3–4 bullets, outcome-framed

```

## 6. Attendee nurture (email-campaigns.md §2D pattern)

Attendees = priority MQL pool (attended live ≥45 min OR clicked replay + deck).
Registrants who didn't attend get the same sequence with "watch on demand."

## 7. Slides template (12–16 slides)

```
1  Title + date + "research reveal" framing
2  Agenda + single-claim statement
3  Method: sources, n, date range, limitations (transparent)
4–8 Data: 4–6 charts, each with 1 takeaway line + source footer
9  Implications for SMB/mid-market (3 blocks)
10 What NOT to do (anti-patterns, honest caveats)
11 Case evidence (anonymized) if available
12 Decision checklist / next step framework
13 CTA slide: single offer + URL
14 Q&A slide + contact line
15 Disclosure + methodology appendix
```

Rules: every chart has a source footer; no chart without a takeaway; ≤6 words
per bullet; brand fonts/colors from `branding.md`; AI-disclosure on every slide.

## 9. Retrospective (monthly, with Euclid)

1. Attendance vs regs; why did people drop?
2. Which data point got the most questions/mentions → repurpose as LinkedIn card + newsletter signal.
3. CTA click-through; which offer converts best.
4. Update topic bank: cut themes with <25% webinar→MQL twice.
5. Log learnings in `13-improvement/experiment-log.md`.

## 10. Tooling & compliance

- Platform: Zoom Webinar (or Google Meet + YouTube live). Recording auto-captioned.
- Captioning on, transcripts offered post-event (accessibility + SEO repurpose).
- Consent checkbox on registration; recording use disclosed; GDPR/CCPA compliant.
- Unsubscribe honored; attendees can opt out of follow-up list with one click.
