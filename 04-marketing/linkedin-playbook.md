# LinkedIn Playbook — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Astra (Marketing) · Goal: authority + reach → MQLs · 3–5 posts/wk.

## 1. Role of LinkedIn in the funnel

LinkedIn is our **authority amplifier**: it distributes research to the exact
buyer (founders, operators, CTOs in 10–500-employee firms) and funnels into
website → magnet → MQL. It is not a broadcast channel — every post must teach,
prove, or open a conversation.

## 2. Profile optimization (owner + company page)

**Owner profile (the human accountability story):**
- Headline: role + outcome promise + evidence cue: "Founder, Meridian Research &
  Advisory — evidence-first AI strategy & market intelligence for SMBs/mid-market."
- Banner: brand colors, tagline "Decisions beyond the horizon.", CTA.
- About: first 3 lines = who we help + what they get + one proof point; then
  services; AI-disclosure line at the end.
- Featured: top 2 reports, 1 benchmark, 1 case study, newsletter link.
- Open to: "Consulting" with services; geographic preference optional.

**Company page:**
- Logo/monogram `M▲`, tagline, website link, industry "Research Services".
- "About" with positioning (`company-brief.md` §positioning) + compliance line.
- Services section: 6 services with URLs (searchable).
- Life tab: only publish content; no fake employee milestones.

## 3. Post formats (rotate weekly)

| Format | Length | Structure | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insight post | 120–200 words, 2–4 short paragraphs | Claim → evidence (source-linked) → implication | 2–3/wk |
| Thread | 4–8 short posts | One technique or 5 findings, numbered, proof in each | 1/wk |
| Data one-pager | Image card (1200×1200 or 1200×628) + short caption | 1 chart/stat + takeaway + source footer | 1/wk |
| Case snippet | 150 words | Challenge → result (numbers) → CTA (consent per case-studies.md) | 1–2/mo |
| Question post | 60–120 words | Ask one sharp question + your data answer | 1/wk |
| Newsletter promo | 100 words | "This week in Meridian Weekly" + signal + subscribe | 1/wk |

### Insight post template
```
H1/first line (hook, 5–8 words): the claim.
Paragraph 2: evidence — named source, number, date, link.
Paragraph 3: what it means for [persona/industry].
1 CTA: comment/consult/download. #hashtags ×2 max (e.g., #AIstrategy #MarketIntel)
```

### Data one-pager card rules
One stat, one takeaway, one source footer, brand fonts/colors (`branding.md`),
chart labeled, no more than 3 colors. File naming: `YYYY-MM-DD-topic-1p.png`.

## 4. 30-day content plan (rolling)

Week cycle (repeat with new topics each month):

| Day | Post | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 8a | Signal of the week (from Meridian Weekly) | Insight post |
| Tue 12p | Engage 10 relevant comments (10 min) | — |
| Wed 9a | Data one-pager (chart from research) | Card |
| Thu 8a | Thread (one technique, 4–6 posts) | Thread |
| Fri 10a | Question post (community input) | Question |
| Sat/Sun | Repost best piece + 1 engagement pass | — |

Monthly: 1 case snippet, 1 newsletter promo, 1 webinar registration post.
**Posting rule:** LinkedIn rewards dwell time — post full insight in the caption
(no "link in comments" gate for organic; keep link in comments optionally).

## 5. Engagement rules

- Reply to every comment within 2h (schedule 2 engagement windows/day, 10–15 min).
- Spend 3× more time commenting on others than posting: comment with substance on
  10–20 relevant posts/wk (target ICP founders/operators + industry voices).
- Tag clients/vendors only when it adds value and they consent.
- Never: buy engagement, post/comment on a purchased list, DM-spam strangers,
  promise guaranteed results, or claim AI capability we can't deliver.
- DM path (only after genuine interaction): "Saw your [x]. We just published data
  on [related]. Happy to send the report — or is a 20-min research brief useful?"

## 6. Lead conversion path

```
Post (insight/card) → comment/DM interest or link click
  → landing page / magnet (consent) → MQL (score ≥40)
  → nurture (Meridian Weekly + magnet follow-up) → consult (Vector)
```

In-post CTA = **one** specific next step. Track via UTM (`utm_campaign=linkedin-YYYY-MM-DD-topic`)
— every post link goes through a tracker so attribution works (`measurement.md` §3).

## 7. Metrics

| Metric | Target | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Posting cadence | 3–5 posts/wk | Weekly check |
| Engagement rate | ≥2% of impressions | Weekly |
| Profile visits /mo | ≥1,500 | Monthly |
| Inbound messages (relevant) | ≥10/mo | Monthly |
| Link clicks → landing page | ≥150/mo | Monthly |
| LinkedIn-attributed MQLs | ≥8/mo | Monthly |
| Follower growth | ≥10%/mo | Monthly |

Kill rule: format with <1.5% engagement for 6 weeks gets replaced (test first).

## 8. Compliance & tone guardrails

- All claims source-linked; AI-disclosure line in profile + report footers.
- No use of client data without consent (case-studies.md §2).
- No scraping DMs; no automated bulk contact.
- Tone: professional, precise, forward-looking — the Sage, not the hype man.
