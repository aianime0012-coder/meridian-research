# Marketing System — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Astra (Marketing) · Demand generation + authority building, fully automated where possible.

## Objective

Build authority and a predictable inbound pipeline that feeds the sales machine,
while compounding Meridian's reputation as an evidence-first AI research firm.

## Pillars

1. **Authority engine** — publish evidence-first research (SEO + thought leadership) to become the default "research firm for AI decisions."
2. **Lead magnet machine** — gated assets that convert visitors into contacts.
3. **Nurture engine** — email/newsletter nurture turning MQLs into sales-ready leads.
4. **Trust layer** — public methodology, QA standards, case studies, and human accountability.

## Channels & tactics

| Channel | Tactic | Cadence | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO | Blog posts, research pages, service pages | 4–8 posts/mo | Organic MQLs |
| LinkedIn | Insight posts, threads, case snippets | 3–5/wk | Authority + reach |
| X/Twitter | Threads, research highlights | 3–5/wk | Reach + community |
| Email | Nurture + newsletter (`newsletter.md`) | Weekly | MQL→SQL |
| Webinars | Monthly themed webinar | Monthly | MQL + demo funnel |
| Lead magnets | Scorecards, snapshots, benchmarks | Quarterly refresh | Conversion |
| Whitepapers/reports | Deep research pieces | Quarterly | Authority + backlinks |
| Videos | Short research explainers | 2–4/mo | Reach + repurposing |

## The content flywheel

```
Research (Sage) → insights → content (Astra) → lead magnets → MQLs
                                                       ↑              │
                                                 nurture/email      Sales (Vector)
                                                       │                │
                                                 case studies ◄───────┘ (feedback)
```

Every client engagement produces a shareable insight (anonymized) that feeds
new content — compounding authority with each delivery.

## Lead magnet library (initial)

- **AI Readiness Scorecard** — self-assessment, email-gated → feeds scoring model.
- **Industry AI Snapshot** (per vertical) — quarterly, gated.
- **AI Adoption Benchmark** — open dataset for SEO/backlinks.
- **Decision-maker's AI Budget Planner** — spreadsheet + guide.
- **Vendor Evaluation Toolkit** — templates for build/buy/partner decisions.

## Measurement (see `measurement.md`)

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Organic sessions/mo | ≥ 5,000 (Y3) |
| MQLs/mo | ≥ 40 |
| MQL→SQL conversion | ≥ 30% |
| Email list growth | ≥ 20%/mo |
| Newsletter open rate | ≥ 45% |
| Content→signup conversion | ≥ 2% |
| Webinar→MQL rate | ≥ 25% |
| Cost per MQL (paid, if used) | ≤ $25 |

## Compliance

- GDPR/CCPA consent on all forms; clear privacy policy link; unsubscribe in every email; no purchased lists for email.

## Weekly rhythm

- Daily: publish/schedule content, reply to engagement, refresh dashboards.
- Weekly: metrics review with Euclid, content calendar update, A/B analysis.
- Monthly: SEO audit, magnet refresh, webinar retrospective, MQL analysis with Sales.
