# Meridian Weekly — Newsletter Spec

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Astra (Marketing) · Weekly Tuesday 7a CT · Open ≥45%, growth ≥20%/mo.

## 1. Positioning

"Meridian Weekly" is the nurture engine and the recurring proof of Meridian's
research engine. It is **not** a digest of links — it is one evidence-first
signal, one insight, one technique, one source. Short, quotable, source-linked.
The AI-readiness scorecard + benchmark data feed it; it feeds MQLs.

## 2. Format (fixed 4 sections)

| Section | Length | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Signal of the week** | 2–3 lines | One verifiable market signal (data point, model release, funding, regulation) with source + date | Sage research stream |
| **2. One insight** | 3–4 lines | What the signal means for SMB/mid-market decision-makers | Astra analysis, owner review |
| **3. One technique** | 4–6 lines | A practical, doable method (e.g., "score vendors in 90 min") linking a magnet or blog post | Astra + magnets |
| **4. One source** | 1–2 lines | One primary source worth reading, linked, with 1-line why | Sage curation |

Plus: **P.S. CTA** — single next step (book consult, download magnet, join webinar).

Rules: every email ≤350 words; every claim source-linked; one CTA; AI-disclosure footer.

## 3. Cadence & timing

- **Frequency:** weekly, every Tuesday, 7a CT (US focus; aligns with Tue–Thu LinkedIn).
- **Draft by:** Monday 12p CT (uses that week's production batch).
- **Sends:** 100% of list if all engaged; segments get topic-matched variants when list >2,000.
- **No send:** skip holiday weeks with a "break week" notice, never ghost.

## 4. Curation process (Research agent → Astra)

```
Sage (daily): scans feeds → drops candidate signals into research queue (06-research/)
Astra (Fri):  picks 3–5 candidates → shortlists 1 signal + 1 source
Owner (Mon):  approves signal, verifies the insight has no overclaim
Astra (Mon):  drafts sections → QA agent checks sources/disclosure
Astra (Tue 6a): schedule; 7a send
Euclid:       log opens/clicks/forwards → dashboard
```

Signal filters: **Relevant to SMB/mid-market** (not enterprise megatrends),
**dated & verifiable**, **actionable**, **no hype** (skip "revolution" stories).
If nothing passes in a week: send the best candidate anyway or publish a "how we
research" note — do not pad with fluff.

## 5. Growth mechanics

| Tactic | Expected lift | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Post-article CTA blocks (every blog) | +5–10% of readers | low |
| Magnet download → auto-subscribe (opt-in checkbox) | +25% of magnet downloads | low |
| Newsletter signup box on every gated landing page | +10–15% | low |
| "Forward this to a colleague" (P.S. line) | +2–5% organic virality | low |
| LinkedIn: post the signal, commenters get newsletter mention | +5–8% | medium |
| Webinar registration includes newsletter signup | +15–20% of webinar regs | low |
| Public archive page (SEO for "meridian weekly") | +2–4% | medium |

**Target:** list grows ≥20%/mo. If growth <15% for 2 months: re-run the magnet
promo, add a popup (respect consent), and A/B the signup form copy.

```
SIGNAL OF THE WEEK

ONE TECHNIQUE

ONE SOURCE

Meridian Research & Advisory · "Decisions beyond the horizon."
Prepared by Meridian Research & Advisory. Analysis is AI-assisted and human-reviewed.

```

## 7. Measurement

| Metric | Target | Action if failing |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | ≥45% | A/B subject; check deliverability (see email-campaigns.md) |
| Click rate | ≥3% | Tighten CTA; cut a section |
| Forward/mention rate | ≥1% | Add forward prompt |
| Unsubscribe | <0.5% | Check list hygiene + content quality |
| Newsletter→MQL | ≥5% of engaged clicks | Strengthen CTA to consult |
| Growth | ≥20%/mo | Run magnet + popup campaigns |

Weekly review every Friday with Euclid. Quarterly: drop lowest-performing section
(reader survey or A/B for 4 weeks), keep strongest.

## 8. Don'ts

- No unlinked claims; no "AI will replace you" doom.
- No more than 1 CTA; no selling every week (keep 3 of 4 issues insight-only).
- No purchased addresses; no sending to non-opted-in segments.
- No changing the 4-section structure casually — test first.
