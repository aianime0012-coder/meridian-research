## Post 1 (Mon) — The 26% number

**Hook:** Most AI investments don't pay off. That's not a hot take — it's a data point.

AI spending is forecast to hit $2.59T this year (+47% YoY, Gartner, May 2026).

But BCG/MIT research puts the share of generative-AI investments that deliver
measurable business value at roughly **26%**.

The gap isn't the models. It's the operating model:

• No named business owner
• No defined success metric
• No kill-switch

62% of stalled enterprise AI projects share one root cause — a missing owner
with budget authority (Alice Labs Implementation Index 2026).

The fix is free: name an owner, define the number, time-box the pilot.

We wrote it up with a 3-step structure — link in comments. What metric would YOU
hold your next AI pilot accountable to?

## Post 2 (Wed) — EU AI Act transparency (timely)

**Hook:** As of this month, the EU AI Act's transparency duties are live.

Article 50 (chatbot disclosure, deepfake labeling, AI-content marking) took
effect **2 August 2026** — and the Digital Omnibus did NOT defer it.

High-risk obligations moved out to Dec 2027 / Aug 2028, but the transparency
layer and the penalty regime (up to €35M or 7% turnover) are enforceable now.

For mid-market teams shipping AI features into EU markets, that means:

• Disclose AI-generated content
• Label chatbots and deepfakes clearly
• Keep a compliance trail

This is exactly the kind of thing that looks like a "later problem" until an
auditor asks. One week to check your transparency posture is a cheap investment.

## Post 3 (Fri) — Agent funding signal (insight-led)

**Hook:** $6B in one quarter is what "agents became real" looks like.

Q3 2026 AI-agent funding crossed $6B, and the money is telling a clear story:

• Infrastructure over finished agents (Prime Intellect $130M, Lyzr $100M)
• Payments rails so agents can move money
• Governance getting funded at scale for the first time — ~$190M across 3 rounds in 11 days

The takeaway for operators: "agents" is becoming an infrastructure category, not
a marketing term. The control layer (what an agent is allowed to touch) is
where investors think the value sits.

If you're advising clients on build/buy/partner this quarter, "agent
governance" belongs in the conversation.

Sources: The Agent Report (Jul 2026), Gravity funding tracker (Jul 2026).
