**Subject:** The 26% problem in AI — Meridian Weekly #001
**Preview:** Most AI bets don't pay off. Here's the fix.

---

**SIGNAL OF THE WEEK**

Only ~26% of generative-AI investments deliver measurable business value,
according to BCG/MIT research. Meanwhile Gartner now forecasts worldwide AI
spending will reach **$2.59 trillion in 2026 — up 47% year-over-year**
([Gartner, May 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-19-gartner-forecasts-worldwide-ai-spending-to-grow-47-percent-in-2026)).

**ONE INSIGHT**

Spending is exploding while value capture lags — and the gap is not technical,
it's structural. A second finding points to the root cause: **62% of stalled
enterprise AI projects have no named business owner** with a defined success
metric and budget authority. For SMBs and mid-market teams, that's the lesson:
before buying more tools, assign a single accountable owner and a measurable
success bar (e.g., "5 hours per rep per week saved"). Tools aren't the
bottleneck; ownership is.

**ONE TECHNIQUE**

**Run a 90-minute vendor score** before any AI purchase. Score candidates
across five categories — capability, cost, data, integration, and risk — with a
weighted rubric (e.g., 25/25/20/15/15). Our free **Vendor Evaluation Toolkit**
gives you the 20-criteria template and scoring sheet, ready in Google Sheets.
Download it, shortlist 3–5 vendors, and kill the spreadsheets-of-features
comparison ritual.

**ONE SOURCE**

[Alice Labs Implementation Index 2026](https://alicelabs.ai/en/insights/ai-adoption-statistics-2026) —
a Nordic dataset measuring pilot-to-production across 100+ enterprise AI builds
(median: 14 weeks, when it works). Read it because it quantifies the failure
modes we keep seeing in the wild.
