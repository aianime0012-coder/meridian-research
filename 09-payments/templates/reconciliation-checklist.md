# Month-End Reconciliation Checklist — Meridian Research & Advisory, LLC

> Aligns with **WF-FN-05** (Finance — Reconciliation). Ref: PAY-REC-01 · Version: 1.0 · Owner: `[Owner / COO]` · Frequency: Monthly, due `[by 5th business day of following month]`

**Purpose:** Ensure every payment, fee, tax, and refund recorded in Meridian OS matches Stripe, PayPal, and the bank, with variance-to-zero before sign-off. Evidence-first: nothing is signed off without matching records.

---

## 1. Pre-Work (Days 1–2)

- [ ] Confirm `[prior month]` is closed and no retroactive edits to the ledger after this run.
- [ ] Pull **Stripe Dashboard exports** for the period: Payments, Disputes, Refunds, Payouts, Fees, Tax (Balance Transactions export).
- [ ] Pull **PayPal** transaction report for the same period.
- [ ] Pull **bank statements** for all accounts receiving payouts/transfers.
- [ ] Export **invoices** auto-generated by Meridian OS for the period.

---

## 2. Stripe vs. Ledger

- [ ] Match every Stripe Payment (charge) to an invoice in the ledger (1:1 via invoice # or metadata `deal_id`).
- [ ] Reconcile payouts to bank deposits: payout amount + payout fees = settled charges for the payout window.
- [ ] Confirm no orphan payments (charges with no matching invoice) — create a follow-up item and assign an owner.
- [ ] Confirm no invoices marked paid without a matching processor charge (manual overrides flagged).

---

## 3. Bank Statements

- [ ] Every Stripe payout deposit matches a payout reference and amount.
- [ ] Every PayPal withdrawal/transfer matches a PayPal settlement.
- [ ] ACH/wire transfers from clients match invoice references.
- [ ] No unexplained deposits or withdrawals; every bank line item is attributable to a source.

---

## 4. Chargebacks & Disputes

- [ ] All disputes listed in Stripe/PayPal are mirrored in the ledger (status `disputed`).
- [ ] Won disputes: funds returned and ledger restored. Lost: charge reversed + fee booked to `[expense account]`.
- [ ] Evidence submitted within `[3]` business days of dispute per PAY-REF-01; confirm none are overdue.

---

## 5. Refunds

- [ ] All refunds this month match an approved request per the refund SOP (approval threshold logged).
- [ ] Refund IDs from the processor match refund entries in the ledger.
- [ ] Partial refunds leave a correct remaining balance on the invoice.
- [ ] No refunds issued outside the documented policy without Owner sign-off.

---

## 6. Fees & Tax

- [ ] Stripe/PayPal processing fees booked: sum of fees = processor fee report totals.
- [ ] Tax collected (sales/VAT) per invoice matches Stripe Tax / tax report; any tax refunded is deducted.
- [ ] Payout net amounts reconcile: gross − fees − refunds − tax = net deposited.

---

## 7. Subscription Anomalies (Stripe Billing)

- [ ] Every subscription is in an expected state: `active`, `trialing`, `past_due`, `canceled`, `paused` — all with a ledger note.
- [ ] **Trialing** accounts flagged: convert to paid on schedule or cancel; none silently forgotten.
- [ ] **Paused** subscriptions verified (no unintended double-billing or missed renewals).
- [ ] Renewals, prorations, and failed-payment (dunning) events match ledger activity.
- [ ] Upgrade/downgrade proration amounts reconciled against plan prices.

---

## 8. Variance-to-Zero

- [ ] Build a variance report: `ledger total − processor total − bank total = 0` (per currency, per account).
- [ ] Every non-zero variance has a documented root cause and a corrective entry.
- [ ] No open variance carried forward without an owner and due date.

---

## 9. Sign-Off

- [ ] Final figures approved by `[Owner / COO]`.
- [ ] Checklist + variance report + all exports archived in `[finance folder / vault]` for the period.
- [ ] Anomalies logged as follow-up tasks with owners and due dates.
- [ ] Month marked **closed** in Meridian OS; prior-period edits locked.

---

## 10. Definitions & References

- **Variance-to-zero:** Final reconciliation where ledger, processor, and bank figures agree with no unexplained difference.
- **References:** WF-FN-05 (Reconciliation), PAY-REF-01 (Refund SOP), PAY-LNK-01 (Payment Link Matrix), Stripe Balance Transactions export guide.
