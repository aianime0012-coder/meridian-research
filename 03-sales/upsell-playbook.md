# Upsell Playbook — Meridian Research & Advisory

**Purpose:** Grow existing accounts profitably. Expansion is cheaper than acquisition and is the primary lever for MRR growth on the Intelligence Subscription, Retainers, and Data & Insight Products.

**Targets:** MRR attach ≥60% at close; ≥20% of existing accounts expand in a 12-month period; average expansion ≥20% of original contract value.

---

## 1. The Upsell Ladder

Every client enters at a rung and can climb. Know the ladder so you know what's next — and never skip rungs.

| Rung | Offer | Entry trigger | Natural next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | AI Strategy Sprint ($5–15k) | New client, project | Add subscription at delivery review |
| 2 | Intelligence Subscription ($1.5–5k/mo) | After Sprint, or as entry | Add advisory or second vertical |
| 3 | Fractional Advisory Retainer ($5–15k/mo) | Subscription clients with recurring questions | Add custom research + retainer |
| 4 | Enterprise Watch (intelligence at scale) | Multiple subscription seats / multi-market | Full intelligence program |
| 5 | Data & Insight Products ($200–2k/mo) | Any client needing ongoing data feeds | Standalone product line, self-serve |

**Ladder rules:**
- One rung at a time. Don't jump a client from Sprint to Retainer; the intermediate step validates value and builds trust.
- The recommended path for new clients is Sprint → Subscription → Advisory. That path is why the proposal bundles Option B (see proposal playbook).
- Products (rung 5) can be cross-sold at any rung as an add-on — they're low-cost, low-friction, and normalize recurring value.

---

## 2. Trigger Events (when to pitch)

Upsell when evidence says the client needs it — not on a calendar timer. Watch these signals:

| Category | Trigger | Pitch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usage | Client is reading every brief, requesting extra scans, asking follow-up questions in >50% of check-ins | Subscription → Advisory |
| Questions asked | "Can you also cover [new market/competitor]?" | Add vertical / expand scope |
| Growth | Client hiring, raising, entering new markets, launching new products (track via news + their own updates) | Enterprise Watch / custom research |
| Internal | New stakeholders added to calls, budget holder signals investment | Retainer |
| Renewal | At renewal, usage high + growth signals | Renew + expand in one proposal |
| Warm leads | Client refers us (referral playbook) — referrer often needs more themselves | Offer next rung as part of the thank-you |

**Anti-signals (do NOT pitch):** active dissatisfaction, recent complaint, slipped deliverables, cost pressure from the client. Fix the relationship first; upsell after trust is restored.

---

## 3. Pitch Timing

Timing rules, in order of priority:

1. **After proof, never before.** First deliverable delivered + positive review = the green light. Before that, no expansion pitches.
2. **Tie to a natural moment:** end of Sprint (delivery review), a check-in where they praised work, a growth event, or renewal.
3. **Match the buyer's calendar:** pitch in the 30–60 days BEFORE their budget closes, not after. If their fiscal year ends Q4, expansion conversations start Q3.
4. **Never in the same breath as a problem** (complaint, delay, invoice dispute). Wait for a clean window.

**Cadence:** one meaningful expansion conversation per quarter per account (Halo owns this beat). Track last-pitch date in CRM.

---

## 4. Pitch Playbook

**Format:** a 15-minute focused call or a 2-page proposal addendum — never a cold email bomb.

**Structure of the pitch (evidence-first, like everything):**
1. Recap the value delivered (cite their usage, the deliverables, the questions they keep asking).
2. Name the gap the data shows (e.g., "you've asked about 3 new markets in 60 days — the subscription covers one").
3. Offer the next rung with a price, scoped and bundled.
4. Ask for the decision, or schedule it with the budget holder.

**Script anchor:**

```
Since March we've delivered X briefs, and you've asked us to extend
into [new market] twice. That's the pattern that tells me the
subscription is now the cheaper version of what you're getting —
same coverage, one price, no project overhead. I'd suggest adding
[vertical] for $[x]/mo. Want me to build that as an addendum?

```

**Pitch do's and don'ts:**
- DO: cite their data (usage, questions, growth).
- DO: bundle (see Section 5) so price feels like value.
- DO: leave a written summary they can forward to finance.
- DON'T: pitch without a trigger event.
- DON'T: sell a rung the client doesn't show signs of needing.
- DON'T: discount to expand — expansion is already low-cost; keep price integrity.

## 5. Packaging & Bundles

Reuse the pricing doc bundles; present expansion as a package, not a line item.

| Bundle | Contents | Price note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subscription + vertical | Intelligence Subscription + 1 new market/vertical | Vertical at 75% of standalone monthly |
| Subscription + advisory day | Subscription + 1 fractional advisory day/month | Advisory day bundled with subscription |
| Retainer + research credits | Advisory retainer + pre-paid custom research credits (e.g., 2 research projects/yr) | Credits at list, retainer anchors |
| Enterprise Watch | Multi-market subscription + quarterly deep-dive + named analyst | Custom quote, CEO approval for new packaging |
| Product add-ons | Data & Insight Products attached to any subscription | Self-serve tiers, one-click add |

**Bundling rules:**
- Every bundle includes at least one recurring element (MRR attach discipline).
- Bundle discount is visible as a line item (transparency, consistent with proposal playbook).
- Bundles never combine two things the client hasn't shown need for.

## 6. Cross-Sell Rules

Cross-sell is allowed and encouraged within defined boundaries:

- **In-boundary:** any Meridian service the client genuinely needs (verified by a trigger event). Products are the safest cross-sell (low cost, low risk).
- **Out-of-boundary:** never sell anything Meridian doesn't deliver well; refer out instead (this is how partner referrals are born — referral playbook).
- **Discipline:** no cross-sell pitch on a call whose agenda is delivery feedback — that's a trust debt. Take the pulse, then pitch in a separate, labeled conversation.
- **Consent:** never share client data with a third party to enable a cross-sell without consent (compliance + trust).
- **MRR priority:** when in doubt between a one-time cross-sell and a subscription, choose the subscription (target ≥60% attach).

**Cross-sell routing:** Halo owns cross-sell discovery (knows the account); AE/deal desk owns the actual sale; the referral program covers anything outside our lane.

## 7. Cadence & Accountability

| Beat | Owner | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Usage + trigger signal scan | Halo | Monthly |
| Expansion conversation | Halo (intro) → AE (close) | Quarterly per account |
| Expansion proposal | AE | When trigger fires |
| Pipeline: expansion % of total | Head of sales | Monthly review |
| Cross-sell / product adds | Halo + AE | Trigger-based |

**CRM hygiene:** every expansion has its own deal record linked to the parent account, with source = `Expansion`, and the trigger event logged. Expansion metrics feed the monthly win/loss review (see win-loss playbook).

**Failure loop:** if an expansion pitch is rejected, log the reason (budget, value, timing, authority) — it feeds both the win/loss data and the renewal playbook's at-risk signals.
