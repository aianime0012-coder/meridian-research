# Outreach Email Templates

> Drafted by Vector + Quill per `03-sales/outreach-playbook.md`.
> Rules enforced: 11 touches / 16 business days; personalized first line; ≤40 cold emails/day (ramp 10→20→30→40); CAN-SPAM — physical address + opt-out in every email; no guaranteed-ROI claims; text-heavy, one link max.
> Placeholders: `[First]`, `[Company]`, `[Insert]` etc. Fill per lead before send. Status: DRAFT.

## Compliance checklist (per email before send)
- [ ] Personalized first line (no mail-merge blanks)
- [ ] Physical postal address + unsubscribe link present
- [ ] No guaranteed-ROI / outcome promises
- [ ] ≤1 link per cold email
- [ ] Inbound scorecard traffic uses nurture (N-*) not cold (E-*)
- [ ] Sending domain passes SPF/DKIM/DMARC; volume ≤ ramp target
