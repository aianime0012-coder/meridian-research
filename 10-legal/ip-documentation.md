# Intellectual Property & Deliverables Documentation

> **TEMPLATE — not legal advice. Have qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction review before execution.**

This document describes the ownership of, and licenses to, intellectual property created, used, or delivered in connection with research and advisory engagements conducted by [Company Legal Name] ("**Company**") for its clients (each, a "**Client**"). This documentation is incorporated by reference into the Master Services Agreement ("**MSA**") and applicable Statements of Work ("**SOWs**"). Capitalized terms not defined herein have the meanings set forth in the MSA.

## 1. Ownership Map

### 1.1 Company Intellectual Property ("Company IP")

As between Company and Client, Company retains all right, title, and interest in and to the following, whether created before or during an engagement, and whether or not incorporated into a Deliverable:

- (a) **Methodology.** Company's proprietary research and analytical methodologies, frameworks, models, workflows, processes, and know-how, including any improvements, enhancements, and derivatives thereof.
- (b) **Templates.** Company's report templates, document structures, interview guides, questionnaire banks, scoring rubrics, and presentation formats.
- (c) **Knowledge Base.** Company's proprietary databases, libraries, source repositories, market data compilations, expert networks, accumulated research, and other aggregated information.
- (d) **Software & Tools.** Company's proprietary software, scripts, algorithms, automation, dashboards, and analytical tools, including source code, object code, and documentation.
- (e) **Pre-Existing Materials.** All materials owned or licensed by Company before commencement of an engagement or developed independently of the engagement.
- (f) **Improvements.** Any enhancements or developments of the foregoing made during the engagement.

### 1.2 Client Intellectual Property ("Client IP")

As between the Parties, Client retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Client Materials, including:

- (a) Data, documents, and information provided by Client to Company;
- (b) Any pre-existing intellectual property of Client incorporated into an engagement; and
- (c) Any deliverables that Client independently develops without use of Company IP.

### 1.3 Deliverables

Deliverables consist of Client-specific work products (e.g., reports, analyses, models) produced for a specific engagement. Deliverables are owned by Client (or licensed to Client) as described in Section 2, subject to Company's retained ownership of any Company IP incorporated therein.

## 2. License to Deliverables

2.1 **Grant.** Subject to full payment of all fees due under the applicable SOW, Company grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, reproduce, and internally distribute the Deliverables for Client's internal business purposes.

2.2 **Scope.** The license includes any Company IP incorporated into the Deliverables, but only to the extent and in the form incorporated therein, and solely for Client's internal business purposes.

2.3 **Conditions.** The license is subject to Client's continued compliance with the MSA, including payment obligations and the confidentiality provisions.

2.4 **No Transferable Rights.** Unless expressly stated in an SOW, the license does not include the right to sublicense, resell, or distribute the Deliverables to third parties.

## 3. Prohibition on Competing Use

3.1 Client shall not use the Deliverables, or any Company IP incorporated therein, to: (a) compete with Company; (b) provide research, analysis, or advisory services to third parties; (c) extract, recombine, or reconstruct Company's methodology or knowledge base; or (d) develop any product or service that substitutes for Company's offerings.

3.2 Company shall not use Client IP except as necessary to perform the Services and shall not disclose Client IP to third parties except as permitted under the MSA.

## 4. Moral Rights

4.1 To the extent permitted by applicable law, and to the extent any rights of attribution, integrity, or other moral rights arise in favor of Company or its personnel in respect of any Deliverable, Company waives such rights to the extent necessary for Client to exercise the license in Section 2.

4.2 Nothing in this Section 4 requires Company to abandon authorship credit for its own Company IP, and Company may continue to use Company IP in the ordinary course of its business.

## 5. Third-Party and Open-Source Components

5.1 Deliverables may incorporate third-party materials, including open-source software, public data sets, licensed databases, and other content. All such third-party materials remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.

5.2 Company shall identify material open-source components and third-party licenses incorporated into a Deliverable where reasonably practicable, either within the Deliverable or in accompanying documentation.

5.3 Nothing in this documentation grants Client rights in third-party materials beyond the rights granted by the applicable third-party licenses. Client is responsible for compliance with such licenses.

## 6. AI-Generated Content Disclosure

6.1 Deliverables are prepared with the assistance of artificial intelligence tools and are subsequently reviewed, edited, and finalized by human subject-matter experts.

6.2 To the extent applicable law confers authorship or ownership on the creator of AI-generated content, and to the extent Company is able to do so, Company assigns or licenses such rights to Client as part of the license in Section 2.

6.3 Client acknowledges that AI-assisted content may be based in part on large-language-model training data and that Company cannot guarantee the provenance of every element of AI-generated content. Company makes no warranty that any AI-generated content is free of third-party rights, except as expressly provided in the MSA.

6.4 Where required by Client's disclosure obligations, Client may, and is encouraged to, identify the Deliverables as AI-assisted and human-reviewed.

## 7. Records of Creation

7.1 Company shall maintain reasonable records of creation for Deliverables, including:

- (a) **Author/Contributor:** names of the individuals who authored, researched, or materially contributed to the Deliverable;
- (b) **Date:** the dates of creation, review, and finalization;
- (c) **Repository/Version:** links or references to the applicable repository, file location, or version control record where the Deliverable is stored; and
- (d) **Sources:** citations and source references for material factual claims, where applicable.

7.2 Such records shall be made available to Client upon reasonable request, subject to the MSA's confidentiality and data protection provisions, and excluding Company's internal process records and knowledge base.

## 8. Inventions and Assignments by Human Contributors

8.1 All human contributors to Deliverables (including Company employees, consultants, and contractors) shall, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law:

- (a) agree in writing that any inventions, works, improvements, or other intellectual property they create within the scope of an engagement are either (i) works made for hire owned by Company, or (ii) assigned to Company; and
- (b) cooperate with Company to perfect, protect, and record Company's rights therein.

8.2 Company shall require its personnel to execute confidentiality, IP assignment, and non-solicitation agreements consistent with this Section 8 as a condition of performing work for Company.

8.3 To the extent any rights described in this Section 8 cannot be assigned, each contributor grants Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, sublicensable license to use such rights in connection with the Deliverables and Company's business.

## 9. Relationship to Other Agreements

9.1 This documentation does not supersede the MSA, SOWs, or the DPA, but supplements them. In the event of a conflict, the order of precedence is: (1) the applicable SOW, (2) the MSA, and (3) this documentation, unless a document expressly states otherwise.

9.2 Nothing in this documentation transfers ownership of Company IP to Client, or Client IP to Company, except as expressly provided herein or in the MSA.
