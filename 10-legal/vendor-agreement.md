# Vendor / Supplier Agreement Template

> **TEMPLATE — not legal advice. Have qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction review before execution.**

This Vendor Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between [Company Legal Name] ("**Company**") and [Vendor Legal Name] ("**Vendor**"). Company and Vendor are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**".

This Agreement governs Vendor's provision of goods and/or services (the "**Vendor Goods/Services**") to Company.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Vendor Goods/Services**" means the goods and/or services described in one or more purchase orders, statements of work, or other ordering documents referencing this Agreement (each, an "**Order**").

1.2 "**Deliverables**" means the work products, software, data, and other materials produced or provided by Vendor for Company under an Order.

1.3 "**Vendor Materials**" means Vendor's pre-existing materials, tools, and intellectual property used to provide the Vendor Goods/Services.

1.4 "**Company Data**" means all data and information provided by or on behalf of Company to Vendor in connection with the Vendor Goods/Services.

1.5 "**Confidential Information**" has the meaning set forth in Section 7.

## 2. Scope of Engagement

2.1 Vendor shall provide the Vendor Goods/Services described in each Order in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards and in accordance with the specifications in the applicable Order.

2.2 Each Order shall include: (a) description of goods/services; (b) specifications and acceptance criteria; (c) delivery dates or schedule; (d) fees; (e) payment terms; and (f) any special terms. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and an Order, this Agreement shall control unless the Order expressly states otherwise.

2.3 Vendor shall not substitute personnel or subcontract goods/services without Company's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld. Vendor remains responsible for the performance of all obligations under this Agreement.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Company shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in each Order. Fees are exclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.

3.2 Payment terms are net [Number] days from receipt of a correct invoice, unless otherwise stated in an Order.

3.3 Invoices shall describe the goods/services provided and shall conform to the requirements of the applicable Order. Company may withhold payment for disputed amounts while the Parties resolve the dispute in good faith.

3.4 Vendor shall be responsible for all taxes (excluding taxes on Company's income) arising from the Vendor Goods/Services, unless the Parties agree otherwise in an Order.

## 4. Delivery and Acceptance

4.1 Vendor shall deliver the Vendor Goods/Services by the dates and to the locations specified in each Order.

4.2 Company shall have [Number] business days after receipt of Deliverables to inspect and test them against the acceptance criteria in the Order (the "**Review Window**").

4.3 If Company identifies non-conformities, it shall notify Vendor in writing within the Review Window, and Vendor shall use reasonable efforts to remedy the non-conformities promptly and re-deliver, with the Review Window resetting.

4.4 If Company does not provide notice of non-conformity within the Review Window, the Deliverables shall be deemed accepted. Acceptance does not waive remedies for latent defects, fraud, or willful misconduct.

## 5. Warranties

5.1 Vendor warrants that: (a) the Vendor Goods/Services will conform in all material respects to the specifications in the applicable Order; (b) the Vendor Goods/Services will be delivered free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of [Number] months from delivery; (c) Vendor has the right and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement; and (d) the Vendor Goods/Services and Vendor Materials, as delivered and used in accordance with this Agreement, do not infringe the intellectual property rights of any third party.

5.2 EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY SET FORTH IN THIS AGREEMENT, VENDOR DISCLAIMS ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED, STATUTORY, OR OTHERWISE, INCLUDING IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

5.3 Company's exclusive remedy for breach of the warranties in this Section 5 shall be repair, replacement, or re-performance, or, if Vendor is unable to do so after a reasonable opportunity, a refund of the fees paid for the non-conforming goods/services, in each case subject to the limitation of liability in Section 9.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 Vendor Ownership. As between the Parties, Vendor retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Vendor Materials and any Vendor pre-existing intellectual property.

6.2 Company Ownership. As between the Parties, Company retains all right, title, and interest in and to any Deliverables created specifically for Company, including any works made for hire, subject to Vendor's license to Vendor Materials under Section 6.3. To the extent any Deliverable is not a work made for hire, Vendor hereby assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in such Deliverable.

6.3 License to Vendor Materials. To the extent any Deliverable incorporates Vendor Materials, Vendor grants Company a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use such Vendor Materials solely as incorporated in the Deliverables, for Company's internal business purposes.

6.4 License to Company Data. Vendor may use Company Data only to provide the Vendor Goods/Services and shall not use or disclose Company Data for any other purpose. Vendor obtains no ownership rights in Company Data.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 Each Party may disclose its Confidential Information to the other Party in connection with this Agreement. "**Confidential Information**" means non-public information disclosed in connection with this Agreement, including business plans, pricing, technology, methodology, Company Data, and data about either Party's customers, regardless of form.

7.2 Each Party shall: (a) use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations under this Agreement; (b) protect it with at least the same degree of care used for its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose it only to personnel and advisors who need to know it and are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Section 7.

7.3 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party before disclosure; (c) is received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed without use of the disclosing Party's information.

7.4 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for [Number] years, except obligations regarding trade secrets continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret.

## 8. Data Protection

8.1 To the extent Vendor processes Personal Data (as defined by applicable law) on behalf of Company, Vendor shall comply with all applicable data protection and privacy laws and with Company's Data Processing Agreement ("**DPA**") or privacy terms referenced in the applicable Order, which are incorporated into this Agreement by reference.

8.2 Vendor shall implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Company Data, including Personal Data, against unauthorized access, loss, use, or disclosure.

8.3 Vendor shall promptly notify Company of any actual or suspected data breach involving Company Data and shall cooperate in mitigation and any required notifications.

8.4 Vendor shall not transfer Company Data to a country or jurisdiction in a manner that would violate applicable data protection laws, unless appropriate safeguards are in place.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

9.1 TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, OR PUNITIVE DAMAGES, OR FOR LOSS OF PROFITS, REVENUE, OR DATA, ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

9.2 EXCEPT FOR (a) EITHER PARTY'S CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS, (b) VENDOR'S INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS, OR (c) COMPANY'S PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS, EACH PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNTS PAID OR PAYABLE BY COMPANY TO VENDOR UNDER THIS AGREEMENT DURING THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

## 10. Indemnification

10.1 Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company from and against third-party claims, damages, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from: (a) a claim that the Vendor Goods/Services or Vendor Materials infringe a third party's intellectual property rights; or (b) Vendor's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of applicable law in performing the Vendor Goods/Services.

10.2 Company shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Vendor from and against third-party claims, damages, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from Company Data or Company's use of the Vendor Goods/Services in violation of this Agreement.

10.3 The indemnified Party shall promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, provide reasonable cooperation, and allow the indemnifying Party to control the defense and settlement, provided that no settlement imposing liability on the indemnified Party shall be made without its prior written consent.

## 11. Termination

11.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated in accordance with this Section 11.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or any Order, for convenience upon [Number] days' prior written notice. Upon termination, Company shall pay for all goods/services delivered or performed through the termination date.

11.3 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for cause if the other Party materially breaches and fails to cure within [Number] days after written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent or ceases operations.

11.4 Sections 1 (Definitions), 6 (Intellectual Property), 7 (Confidentiality), 8 (Data Protection), 9 (Limitation of Liability), 10 (Indemnification), 12 (Governing Law), and any other provisions that by their nature should survive, shall survive termination.

## 12. Governing Law

13.2 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.

13.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement, together with all Orders, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.

13.4 **Severability.** If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
