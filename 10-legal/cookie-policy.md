## 2. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

- **Cookies** are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to recognize your device and remember information about your visit.
- **Local storage** and **session storage** store data in your browser without transmitting it to us with every request, but serve similar purposes.
- **Pixels / trackers** are small snippets of code that collect information about site usage or interactions.

Some cookies are set by us ("first-party cookies") and some by third parties we work with ("third-party cookies").

## 5. How to Control and Delete Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

- Block or allow all cookies;
- Block third-party cookies only;
- Delete cookies when you close the browser;
- Receive a notification when a cookie is set; or
- Manage specific cookies per site.

Links to manage cookies in common browsers:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
- **Edge:** Settings → Cookies and site permissions

You can also opt out of analytics tracking using the tools provided by the analytics providers themselves (e.g., the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on at [https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout]). Because analytics data in Plausible is aggregated and does not use cookies, it does not require cookie-based opt-out.

Note: Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Site, and disabling all cookies (including strictly necessary ones) may prevent certain features from working.
