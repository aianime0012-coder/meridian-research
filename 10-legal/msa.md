# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

This Master Services Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between [Company Legal Name] ("**Company**"), a company organized under the laws of [State/Jurisdiction], with its principal place of business at [Address], and [Client Legal Name] ("**Client**"), with its principal place of business at [Client Address]. Company and Client are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**".

This Agreement governs all Services performed by Company for Client under Statements of Work entered into pursuant to this Agreement. In the event of a conflict between this Agreement and any Statement of Work, the terms of this Agreement shall control unless the Statement of Work expressly states that it modifies a specific provision of this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**Services**" means the research, analysis, advisory, consulting, and related professional services described in one or more Statements of Work.

1.2 "**Statement of Work**" or "**SOW**" means a written statement of work referencing this Agreement, executed by both Parties, describing the Services, deliverables, fees, milestones, and schedules.

1.3 "**Deliverables**" means the work products, reports, analyses, documents, models, data compilations, and other materials produced by Company for Client under an SOW and expressly designated as deliverables therein.

1.4 "**Client Materials**" means all data, documents, information, and materials provided by or on behalf of Client to Company in connection with the Services.

1.5 "**Company Materials**" means Company's proprietary methodology, templates, frameworks, analytical tools, software, knowledge base, pre-existing materials, and any other materials owned by Company or its licensors that are used in performing the Services, whether or not incorporated into a Deliverable.

1.6 "**Confidential Information**" means all non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement, including business plans, financial information, customer data, research, models, methodology, and know-how, regardless of form, as further described in Section 9.

1.7 "**Services Fee**" means the fees set forth in an SOW for the Services and Deliverables described therein.

## 2. Services

2.1 Company shall perform the Services and provide the Deliverables described in each SOW in a professional and workmanlike manner, consistent with industry standards for research and advisory services of a similar nature.

2.2 Each SOW shall describe: (a) the Services to be performed; (b) the Deliverables and acceptance criteria; (c) the milestones and schedule; (d) the Services Fee and payment schedule; (e) Client responsibilities and assumptions; and (f) any special terms.

2.3 Company may use subcontractors or independent professionals to perform portions of the Services, provided that Company remains responsible for the performance of the Services and compliance with this Agreement.

2.4 Company does not guarantee any specific business, financial, or operational outcome from the Services. All research, forecasts, and recommendations are provided for informational and decision-support purposes only.

## 3. Fees and Payment

3.1 Fees. Client shall pay Company the Services Fees and any other amounts set forth in each SOW.

3.2 Payment Terms. Unless otherwise stated in an SOW, all invoices are due net fifteen (15) days from the date of invoice ("**Net-15**").

3.3 Late Payments. Any amount not paid when due shall accrue interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less, from the due date until paid.

3.4 Invoicing. Company shall invoice Client in accordance with the invoicing schedule in the applicable SOW. Each invoice shall describe the Services performed and fees due.

3.5 Taxes. All fees are exclusive of taxes. Client shall be responsible for all sales, use, value-added, and similar taxes (excluding taxes on Company's net income) arising from the Services. If Company is required to collect or remit any such tax, Company shall invoice Client for the applicable amount, and Client shall pay it.

3.6 Expenses. Unless otherwise stated in an SOW, travel and out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Company in performing the Services shall be pre-approved in writing by Client and reimbursed in accordance with Company's standard expense policies.

3.7 Disputed Amounts. Client may dispute an invoice in good faith in writing within fifteen (15) days of receipt. The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve the dispute. Undisputed portions of an invoice remain payable when due.

## 4. Intellectual Property and Licenses

4.1 Company IP. As between the Parties, Company retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Company Materials, including any portions of Company Materials incorporated into a Deliverable.

4.2 Client IP. As between the Parties, Client retains all right, title, and interest in and to the Client Materials.

4.3 License to Deliverables. Subject to full payment of all fees due under the applicable SOW, Company grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide license to use, reproduce, and internally distribute the Deliverables for Client's internal business purposes.

4.4 License to Company Materials. To the extent a Deliverable incorporates Company Materials, the license in Section 4.3 includes a limited right to use such Company Materials solely as incorporated in the Deliverable, and solely for Client's internal business purposes. Client may not extract, recombine, or independently use Company Materials outside the Deliverables.

4.5 Prohibition on Competing Use. Client shall not resell, sublicense, redistribute, or use the Deliverables or Company Materials to compete with Company or to provide services to third parties, except as expressly permitted in an SOW.

4.6 Client Feedback. Any suggestions, improvements, or feedback Client provides regarding the Services or Company Materials may be used by Company without obligation to Client.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Each Party may disclose its Confidential Information to the other Party in connection with this Agreement.

5.2 Each Party shall: (a) use the other Party's Confidential Information only to perform its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement; (b) protect such Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; and (c) disclose such Confidential Information only to those of its personnel and advisors who need to know it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Section 5.

5.3 Exclusions. Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the receiving Party; (b) was lawfully known to the receiving Party without restriction before disclosure; (c) is rightfully received from a third party without obligation of confidentiality; or (d) is independently developed by the receiving Party without use of the disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

5.4 Required Disclosure. A Party may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation, or court order, provided it gives the disclosing Party reasonable advance notice (where permitted) and uses reasonable efforts to obtain confidential treatment.

5.5 Return or Destruction. Upon the other Party's request or termination of this Agreement, each Party shall return or destroy the other Party's Confidential Information, except as required by law or as necessary to comply with legal or regulatory obligations, and shall certify such return or destruction upon request.

5.6 Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for a period of [Number] years, except that obligations regarding trade secrets continue for as long as such information remains a trade secret.
