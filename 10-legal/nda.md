# Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (NDA)

> **TEMPLATE — not legal advice. Have qualified counsel in the relevant jurisdiction review before execution.**

This Mutual Nondisclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of [Effective Date] by and between [Company Legal Name] ("**Company**"), with its principal place of business at [Address], and [Counterparty Name] ("**Counterparty**"), with its principal place of business at [Counterparty Address]. Company and Counterparty are each referred to as a "**Party**" and together as the "**Parties**".

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship (the "**Purpose**") and may disclose Confidential Information to one another in connection therewith.

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

1.1 "**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information, whether oral, written, electronic, or in any other form, disclosed by a disclosing Party ("**Discloser**") to a receiving Party ("**Recipient**") in connection with the Purpose, including without limitation:

- (a) Business, financial, and operational information, including revenue, pricing, costs, customers, and suppliers;
- (b) Technical information, including methodology, models, algorithms, research, analyses, and know-how;
- (c) Strategic plans, business plans, forecasts, and prospective transactions;
- (d) Personal data and other regulated information, to the extent applicable law requires protection;
- (e) Proprietary software, source code, documentation, data, and databases;
- (f) Any other information identified as confidential at the time of disclosure or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

1.2 Confidential Information may be disclosed orally or visually, in which case Discloser shall reduce it to writing and mark it "Confidential" within [Number] days of disclosure. Information that a reasonable person would recognize as confidential is protected even if not so identified.

1.3 Information disclosed by one Party's personnel, affiliates, or advisors on its behalf shall be treated as that Party's Confidential Information.

## 2. Exclusions

2.1 Confidential Information does not include information that Recipient can demonstrate:

- (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by Recipient;
- (b) was lawfully in Recipient's possession before disclosure by Discloser, without obligation of confidentiality;
- (c) is rightfully received by Recipient from a third party without an obligation of confidentiality and without breach of this Agreement; or
- (d) is independently developed by Recipient without use of or reference to Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

3.1 Recipient shall:

- (a) use Discloser's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
- (b) protect Discloser's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, and in no event less than reasonable care;
- (c) disclose Discloser's Confidential Information only to those of its personnel and advisors who need to know it for the Purpose, who are informed of its confidential nature, and who are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less restrictive than those in this Agreement; and
- (d) not disclose, copy, or use Discloser's Confidential Information for any other purpose.

3.2 Recipient is responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its personnel and advisors to whom it discloses Confidential Information.

3.3 Recipient may make copies of Discloser's Confidential Information only to the extent reasonably necessary for the Purpose. Such copies are Discloser's Confidential Information and subject to this Agreement.

## 4. Term

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until terminated by either Party upon [Number] days' prior written notice, or until the Purpose is completed, whichever occurs first.

4.2 The obligations in this Agreement regarding Confidential Information that constitutes trade secrets shall continue for as long as such information remains a trade secret under applicable law. All other obligations regarding Confidential Information shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years from the date of termination or from the date of disclosure, whichever is later.

## 5. Return or Destruction

5.1 Upon Discloser's request or upon termination of this Agreement, Recipient shall, at Discloser's option, return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession, including all copies and extracts, and shall certify in writing that such return or destruction has occurred within [Number] business days.

5.2 Recipient may retain copies as required by law or its bona fide record-keeping policies, provided such retained copies remain subject to this Agreement and are not used for any other purpose.

## 6. No License Grant

6.1 Nothing in this Agreement grants either Party any right, title, or interest in or to the other Party's Confidential Information or intellectual property. All Confidential Information remains the property of Discloser. Recipient obtains no license to use Discloser's Confidential Information except the limited right to use it for the Purpose as set forth in this Agreement.

## 7. No Reverse Engineering

7.1 Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code, underlying algorithms, methodology, structure, or logic of any confidential software, models, or materials disclosed by Discloser.

7.2 Recipient shall not use Discloser's Confidential Information to develop, improve, or validate any competing product, methodology, or service, except as expressly authorized by Discloser in writing.

## 8. Remedies

8.1 The Parties acknowledge that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. Accordingly, Discloser shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief in any court of competent jurisdiction, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

8.2 No failure to exercise or delay in exercising any right under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof.

## 9. No Obligation to Disclose or Transact

9.1 Neither Party is obligated to disclose any particular information under this Agreement, and neither Party is obligated to enter into any transaction with the other. This Agreement does not create a joint venture, partnership, agency, or employment relationship.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [State/Jurisdiction], without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings, whether oral or written.

11.2 **Amendment.** This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both Parties.

11.3 **Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.

11.4 **Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

11.5 **Notices.** All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in the signature blocks below.
