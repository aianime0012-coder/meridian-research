## 1. Purpose and How to Use

This register identifies key operational, financial, legal, and strategic risks facing the Company, along with owners, mitigation, and contingency plans. It is a living document reviewed quarterly and updated on material changes.

## 2. Scoring Guide

### 2.1 Likelihood
| Rating | Score | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 1 | Unlikely to occur (e.g., <10% in 12 months) |
| Medium | 2 | Possible; may occur (e.g., 10–50% in 12 months) |
| High | 3 | Likely or expected (e.g., >50% in 12 months) |

### 2.2 Impact
| Rating | Score | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 1 | Minor disruption; manageable cost; no strategic impact |
| Medium | 2 | Moderate financial/operational/reputational impact; recoverable with effort |
| High | 3 | Severe impact (revenue loss, legal exposure, major reputational damage, business continuity risk) |

### 2.3 Score = Likelihood × Impact
| Score | Band | Priority | Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Green | Low | Monitor; reassess quarterly |
| 3–4 | Amber | Medium | Active mitigation owner; review each quarter |
| 6–9 | Red | High | Immediate mitigation; escalate to leadership; review monthly |

**Risk appetite:** The Company generally accepts Low (green) risks without active treatment. Amber risks require a documented mitigation plan. Red risks require immediate action and leadership sign-off.

## 4. Risk Assessment Notes

- **Inherent vs. residual:** the register reflects residual risk after mitigation. When scoring, note the risk before and after mitigations are applied.
- **Escalation:** any risk scoring 6 or above requires owner sign-off at the next leadership review and a documented mitigation plan.
- **New risks:** add new risks as they emerge; assign an owner at the time of addition and place on the next review agenda.

## 5. Quarterly Review Process

1. **Review cadence:** the full register is reviewed **quarterly** (every 3 months) by the leadership team, with a monthly light-touch check of Red risks.
2. **Activities during review:**
   - Re-score all risks against current conditions;
   - Confirm owners, mitigations, and contingency plans are current;
   - Verify evidence of mitigation actions (see Compliance Checklist);
   - Add new risks and close resolved ones (keep history in Section 7);
   - Report top risks (score ≥ 6) to leadership and record decisions.
3. **Trigger-based review:** conduct an off-cycle review on any material change (e.g., major client loss, breach, regulatory change, new service line, or significant contract).
