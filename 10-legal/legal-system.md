# Legal System — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Amicus (Legal) + Solon (Compliance) · Templates prepared; **legally binding execution is always a human (owner) gate.**

## Posture

- All contracts and policies are **prepared templates** with clear `[Placeholders]`.
- Agents draft, review, and route. **No agent signs, executes, or files anything legally binding.**
- Owner reviews/executes every contract; owner engages licensed counsel where required (filing, litigation, cross-border).
- Templates are jurisdiction-neutral by default; Solon flags jurisdiction-specific requirements.

## Workflow

1. **Need detected** (proposal accepted, vendor engaged, data shared) → Amicus selects template.
2. Amicus + Solon complete placeholders, review compliance flags.
3. QA gate (Norm) checks completeness.
4. Route to owner for review/execution (e-sign via DocuSign/PandaDoc).
5. Executed copy → contract register (`11-knowledge/decision-log/`) + finance (for invoicing) + security (for data clauses).

## Key legal rules the company operates under

- **No guarantees of specific outcomes** in marketing or sales (false advertising risk).
- **AI-assisted disclosure** on deliverables; humans accountable for legal output.
- **Data protection:** GDPR/CCPA-ready; DPAs before processing client data; minimize PII.
- **Money:** only via licensed processors; never hold/reroute client funds.
- **IP:** deliverables license to client on payment; Meridian methodology/knowledge remains Meridian IP.
- **Records:** 7-year retention for financial/legal documents.

## Escalation

- Anything creating legal obligation or liability → Amicus review → Owner execution.
- Regulatory inquiry / subpoena / dispute → Owner immediately; counsel engaged.
- AI Act / privacy uncertainty → Solon research memo before proceeding.
