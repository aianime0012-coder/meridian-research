## 1. Purpose and How to Use

This checklist organizes the Company's standing compliance obligations by area. It is a working document, not a substitute for legal advice. Each item identifies the **obligation**, the **owner**, the **cadence** for review/renewal, and the **evidence** that demonstrates compliance. Applicability of each item depends on the Company's operations, jurisdictions, client base, and applicable law — where an item does not apply, mark it N/A and note the reason.

Cadence key: **C** = continuous/ongoing, **M** = monthly, **Q** = quarterly, **Y** = annual, **E** = event-driven.
