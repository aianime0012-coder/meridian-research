## Cross-department principles

- **Memory:** every agent writes to the knowledge base (`11-knowledge/`) and the company DB; no agent works from scratch.
- **Handoffs:** an agent's output is the next agent's input (documented in each blueprint's Inputs/Outputs).
- **Escalation:** routine → department head → COO → CEO → (legally binding) → Owner.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** only legally binding acts, entity/financial setup, and accounts require the owner. Everything else is autonomous.
