# SOP — Training & Enablement

> Version 1.0 · Owner: Dispatch (Delivery Ops) + Halo (Client Success) · Applies to: Training & Enablement ($2–8k, 1–2 day workshops)

## Purpose

Deliver practical AI workshops and enablement packs (e.g., "AI literacy for leaders", "Prompt engineering for analysts", "AI vendor evaluation playbook") that equip client teams to act on Meridian research and AI adoption. Outcomes are measured: post-workshop assessment, materials pack, and follow-up adoption review.

## Modules

- **AI literacy for leaders** — fundamentals, value drivers, governance, buyer's view of AI investment.
- **Prompt engineering for analysts** — effective prompting, retrieval use, validation of outputs.
- **AI vendor evaluation playbook** — evaluation criteria, demo scoring, TCO and risk review.
- Additional modules scoped on request; each module ships with exercises, cheat sheets, and an assessment.
- Module selection follows the audience assessment captured at kickoff.

## Trigger

- Signed SOW (`MRA-<client>-training-<date>`) + deposit invoice paid (Tally); Legal Gate closed by Amicus.
- Client confirmed workshop dates, format (remote/onsite), audience, and size (target 5–25 attendees).
- Attach offer (intelligence subscription or advisory retainer) quoted per pricing policy.

## Prerequisites

- Signed SOW + payment terms; workshop dates confirmed in calendar; venue/link verified.
- Workspace created by Dispatch: `MRA-<client>-training-<YYYYMMDD>` with folders (`00-intake/`, `10-curriculum/`, `20-materials/`, `30-qa/`, `40-delivery/`), portal, and shared drive for materials.
- Audience assessment captured by Halo: participant roles, AI experience level, tools in use, and specific enablement goals.
- Curriculum template selected by module (leader / analyst / vendor-evaluation); draft curriculum assigned to an authoring agent.
- Materials pack components identified: slide deck, exercises, cheat sheets, templates, and assessment instrument.

## Steps

1. **Kickoff call** — Halo + Kepler. Confirm audience, goals, format, dates, and logistics; collect attendee list and access needs. Log to the requirements brief; post to portal. *Outcome: curriculum scope confirmed by client.*
   - Agenda: audience, goals, format, logistics, assessment expectations, materials access.
   - Attendance list and roles shared by client; any access (LMS/venue/link) provisioned by Dispatch.
2. **Curriculum design** — Sage + Kepler. Design the curriculum by module: learning objectives, session plan (timing per segment), exercises, and assessment. Align every module to the audience assessment and client goals. *Output: curriculum plan.*
   - Each module maps objective → content → exercise → assessment check.
3. **Materials production** — Sage. Build the materials pack: facilitator deck, participant handouts, exercise workbooks, cheat sheets, and templates. Every technical claim source-linked and grounded in Meridian research. *Output: materials draft.*
   - Exercise answer keys and facilitator talking points included.
4. **Assessment design** — Sage + Norm. Build the pre/post workshop assessment (knowledge + confidence) matched to learning objectives, plus a facilitator observation rubric. *Output: assessment instruments.*
5. **QA gate** — Norm. Run the training QA checklist: factual accuracy, source verification, pedagogical soundness (objectives → content → assessment alignment), no promotional hype, brand voice, formatting, and accessibility checks. *Gate: no client-facing materials before Norm approval.*
   - Accessibility: readable fonts, alt text on visuals, formats usable by all attendees.
6. **Facilitation prep** — Dispatch + Sage. Finalize facilitation notes: timing, interactive segments, likely questions with answers, and contingency activities. Brief the facilitator. *Gate: facilitator brief complete ≥ 48h before the workshop.*
7. **Workshop delivery** — Sage (facilitator) + Halo. Deliver per the session plan. Pre-workshop assessment administered at session start; exercises run on schedule; participant questions logged. *Gate: attendance and assessment completion recorded.*
   - Timing kept per plan; contingency activities used if a segment runs short.
8. **Post-workshop assessment** — Sage. Administer the post-workshop assessment; score pre/post deltas; compile results against learning objectives. *Output: assessment results.*
9. **Materials pack delivery** — Dispatch. Deliver the full materials pack (slides, workbooks, cheat sheets, templates, assessment summary) via the portal within 3 business days of the workshop. *Gate: client acknowledges receipt.*
10. **Follow-up assessment** — Halo. At 30 days, run the follow-up: usage check (are participants applying the skills?), short impact survey, and adoption blockers. Report to the client with next-step recommendations. *Gate: follow-up report delivered; lessons captured.*
11. **Feedback capture** — Halo. Workshop CSAT (reaction) at session end and impact feedback at 30 days. Route lessons to Mnemos and Norm; hand upsell triggers to Vector. *Gate: both feedback points recorded before close.*
12. **Archive** — Mnemos. Store curriculum, materials, assessment templates, and lessons in the knowledge base; index reusable modules for future engagements. Dispatch archives per `project-lifecycle.md`; Tally reconciles the invoice. *Gate: archive checklist complete.*

## Deliverables

1. **Workshop delivery** — 1–2 day facilitated session per the curriculum plan.
2. **Materials pack** — facilitator deck, handouts, exercises, cheat sheets, templates.
3. **Assessment results** — pre/post deltas and 30-day follow-up report.

**Facilitation notes** (Sage): per-module timing, interactive segments, likely questions with answer keys, and contingency activities; facilitator brief completed ≥ 48h before the workshop.

**Assessment design** (Sage + Norm): pre/post knowledge + confidence items mapped 1:1 to learning objectives; scoring rules defined before delivery so deltas are comparable; facilitator observation rubric records participation and blockers.

**QA checklist** (Norm): technical claims source-verified; objectives–content–assessment alignment; no promotional/guarantee language; exercises executable as written; materials accessible (readability, formats); brand voice; versioned and dated.

## KPIs

| KPI | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery | Workshop + materials on contracted dates | ≥ 95% |
| QA first-pass rate | Materials passing QA gate first try | ≥ 90% |
| CSAT | Workshop reaction score (1–5) | ≥ 4.5 |
| Learning gain | Avg. post vs. pre assessment delta | ≥ +20% |
| 30-day application | % of participants applying skills at follow-up | ≥ 60% |
| Time-to-first-value | Sign → workshop delivered | ≤ 14 days |
| Attach rate | Training → subscription/retainer conversion | ≥ 40% |
| Remediation rate | Low-scoring modules offered a fix within follow-up | 100% |

## Escalation

- Curriculum/materials QA failure → Norm blocks; fixes owned by Sage; release override requires COO+CEO.
- Workshop logistics failure (venue, connectivity, facilitator unavailable) → Dispatch → COO; client notified with contingency plan within 4h.
- Scope change (audience growth, extra modules) → Kepler change control; price/date impact → COO + Vector; contract amendment → Amicus + Owner.
- Participant dissatisfaction or safety/complaint during workshop → Halo escalates to COO within 24h.
- Confidential client data shared during exercises → Sentinel + Amicus.
- Low post-assessment scores (< 50% of objectives met) → Halo + Sage offer a remediation path within the follow-up report (repeat module or office-hours session).
- Recurring module-level QA findings → Sage updates the module template and Norm refreshes the checklist within 5 days.
