# Sales Scripts — Playbook

> **Owner:** Meridian Knowledge (11-knowledge) · **Version:** 1.0
> **Brand voice:** Evidence-first, calm, consultative. We sell decisions, not time. Never overpromise; let the source-linked track record carry the close.
> **Core rule:** Every conversation ends with a confirmed next step (meeting, document, or date). No "let's circle back later."

---

## 1. Discovery Call — 30-Minute Script

**Goal:** Qualify fit, surface the decision they're facing, and book the next step. Speak <40% of the time.

### 0:00–2:00 — Open & frame
> "Hi [Name], thanks for the time. I've [read / skimmed] [their context if any]. Today I want to understand the decisions your team is facing around [AI / intelligence / research / capability], where the pressure is coming from, and whether Meridian can help. At the end I'll share one honest view — even if the answer is 'not us.' Sound fair?"

### 2:00–8:00 — Current state (S)
- "What prompted you to take this call / look at this now?"
- "What is the team currently doing for [the function] today — tools, people, outside help?"
- "What's working well? What frustrates you most about it?"
- **Listen for:** a stated trigger event, an owner, an existing budget line.

### 8:00–14:00 — AI initiatives (A)
- "What has the organization already tried or piloted with AI?"
- "What's the appetite for autonomous agents vs. augmented tools?"
- "Who's driving that agenda internally, and who would need to approve it?"
- **Qualifier:** Are they experimenting or committed? Committed = hotter.

### 14:00–19:00 — Budget & timeline (B)
- "If the fit is right, what does the budget look like — is there a line item or would it need to be carved out?"
- "What's the timeline? Is something forcing a date (board, quarter, competitor, regulation)?"
- "What's the cost of *not* doing anything in the next two quarters?"
- **Qualifier:** Budget + timeline both real? Then qualify further. Neither? Lower priority.

### 19:00–24:00 — Decision process (D)
- "How will a decision like this get made — who's involved, who has the final call?"
- "What evidence or validation would make you confident to move?"
- "Who else should be in the next conversation?"
- **Qualifier:** Identify the champion, the economic buyer, and the blockers.

### 24:00–28:00 — Next step
- "Based on this, here's the fit I see: [strength / gap]. I'd suggest [next step]. Which works better — [option A] or [option B]?"
- **Next steps:** book a kickoff-style deep dive, send a tailored proposal, or schedule a decision review with the broader buyer group.

### 28:00–30:00 — Close
- "I'll send [artifact] by [date]. For the diary, you'll have [date] reserved. Anything else I should know before we talk?"

## 2. Qualification Questions (fit check)

Rate 0–2 each; score ≥7/10 to pursue, 4–6 to nurture, <4 to release.

1. **Pain:** Is there a concrete, stated problem or decision? (0–2)
2. **Authority:** Can they say yes / do they influence the yes? (0–2)
3. **Budget:** Is there a line item or realistic carve-out? (0–2)
4. **Timeline:** Is there a forcing function with a date? (0–2)
5. **Fit:** Does our service actually match their need (evidence vs. research vs. advisory)? (0–2)
6. **Access:** Will they share sources, data, and stakeholders? (0–2)

**Red flags:** "Just send pricing" with no context · no named problem · can't name the decision-maker · expects guaranteed results · refuses any evidence sharing.

## 3. Objection Responses

### "It's too expensive."
> "That's exactly the conversation we should have — the question isn't the fee, it's the cost of inaction. In our [engagement/case study], the decision this work enabled was worth [quantified value]. If I can show you that the research pays for itself on one decision, would the budget become available?"

### "We can do this with our internal team."
> "Good — internal teams often have context we don't. The question is opportunity cost: is your team's best use of 8–12 weeks the deep research, or the execution of the decision? We partner *with* internal teams — they own the decision, we compress the evidence-gathering. What would your team rather be doing?"

### "Not now / timing isn't right."
> "Understood. Let's be specific — is it budget, bandwidth, or that the problem isn't urgent enough yet? [If not urgent:] I'll leave you with one indicator: when [X] happens, that's your trigger. Can I put a note to revisit [quarter]? In the meantime, I'll send a one-page brief you can keep."

### "Why an AI-first research firm?"
> "Three reasons: speed, traceability, and honesty. Our agents run wider source coverage in days than a manual team does in months, every claim carries a source link, and everything is QA-gated and human-reviewed before it reaches you. You get breadth plus accountability — that combination is rare."

### "How fast can you deliver?"
> "For [service], typical delivery is [X] weeks from kickoff, with a milestone plan we sign off together. The honest constraint is your inputs — the sooner you share sources and stakeholders, the sooner you get evidence. When would you want the first milestone in hand?"

## 4. Meeting Scheduling Scripts

### Cold email opener (short version)
> "Subject: [Their trigger, e.g., 'Your Q3 AI decision'] — 20 minutes
> Hi [Name], seeing [context]. We help firms like yours make evidence-based AI decisions. Worth 20 minutes to see if we can compress your [problem]? I'll bring a view either way. — [Your name]"

### Warm referral follow-up
> "Hi [Name], [Referrer] suggested we talk about [topic]. I've got [day + time] or [day + time] open. What suits?"

### Scheduling confirmation (send meeting link)
> "Confirmed: [date + time]. Agenda: current state (5'), your AI initiatives (5'), budget/timeline (5'), decision process (5'), and an honest fit read (5'). Here's [calendar link]. If anything changes, just ping me."

## 5. Follow-Up Email Scripts

### Post-call summary (send within 24h)
> "Hi [Name], thanks again. Quick recap of what we covered: [3 bullets]. What I heard: [their problem], with [trigger]. Next step we agreed: [X] by [date]. Attached: [relevant source-linked brief]. Worth scheduling the follow-up? — [Name]"

### Nurture (no reply, 7 days)
> "Hi [Name], following up on [topic]. One thing that might help while you decide: [one source-linked insight]. Happy to hop on a call if useful — otherwise I'll check in [month]."

### Break-up (after 2–3 no-replies)
> "Hi [Name], I'll close this thread out. If [topic] resurfaces, I've left [resource] here and you know where to find me. Best of luck with [their goal]."

### Proposal sent
> "Hi [Name], proposal attached. Three things worth your attention: [A], [B], [C]. I'll follow up on [date] — happy to walk through any of it before then."

## 6. Renewal Conversation Script (Intelligence Subscription / Retainer)

**When:** 60 days before renewal. **Rule:** always lead with demonstrated value, never with the price.

> "Hi [Name], we're ~60 days from your [subscription/retainer] renewal. Before we talk logistics, I want to confirm the value it produced. In the last year, you've used our output to [3 concrete uses]. Which of those mattered most?
> If we were designing the next [12 months] from scratch, what would you add, drop, or change? [Listen.] Would you like me to adjust scope or focus for the next term?
> If it's a yes: 'I'll send a one-page renewal summary with the agreed changes by [date] — you'll have [terms] to review. Any questions?'

**Renewal objection — "we didn't use it enough":**
> "That's the most useful thing you could tell me. Let's fix usage before we fix the contract — I'll propose a lighter-touch integration plan for the next 90 days, and we re-evaluate together. If it still isn't earning its keep, we'll downsize cleanly. Fair?"

## 7. Script Discipline Notes

- **Always record a next step** — a meeting, an artifact, or an explicit release ("I'll follow up in Q4").
- **Never invent evidence** — if you can't source it, don't claim it. Refer the prospect to a source-linked artifact.
- **Close loop in CRM:** every call logged with qualification score within 24h.
- **Transfer to delivery:** hand off call notes + qualification score to the engagement owner at SETUP.
