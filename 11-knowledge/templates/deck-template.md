## Slide-by-Slide Outline

### 1. Title Slide
- Deck title, client logo, Meridian branding, date, confidentiality mark.
- **Guidance:** No agenda clutter. One line stating the decision this deck enables.

### 2. Agenda
- 5–7 items mirroring the sections below.
- **Guidance:** Keep it boring and predictable — executives want to know the path.

### 3. Context / Problem
- The business context in 3 bullets or less.
- **The problem** stated as a decision to be made, not a symptom.
- **Cost of inaction** — what happens if nothing changes, quantified where possible.
- **Guidance:** This slide earns attention. Use the client's own language and a concrete trigger event ("since Q1 X…", "after the [event]…").

### 4. Evidence Landscape
- **What we found:** 2–4 headline evidence points, each with source citation `[S#]`.
- **What we did not find:** explicit gaps and low-confidence areas.
- **Guidance:** Build trust by showing the evidence *before* the conclusion. Every bullet cites a source (see appendix). This is the QA-critical slide.

### 5. Evidence — Detail (optional, max 1–2 slides)
- Charts or tables for the 2–3 most decision-relevant data points.
- Each visual gets a one-sentence takeaway in the title (e.g., "Competitors cut time-to-market by 40% within 18 months").
- **Guidance:** One number per visual. If it needs explanation, cut the visual.

### 6. Interpretation
- What the evidence means for this client specifically (not a general summary).
- Link to their stated objectives and constraints.
- **Guidance:** This is where Meridian adds judgment — label clearly as analysis, keep it grounded in slide 4.

### 7. Opportunity
- The framed opportunity: **size, timing, window of action, fit with client strategy.**
- Indicators that would confirm or kill the opportunity.
- **Guidance:** Frame as "what the client stands to gain / lose by when." Stay honest about uncertainty.

### 8. Options — Build / Buy / Partner
- 3 columns: **Build** · **Buy** · **Partner**, each with:
  - Description · cost (capex/opex) · timeline · risk · evidence of comparable outcomes `[S#]`.
- **Guidance:** Neutral presentation. Strengths and weaknesses for all three — do not stack the deck yet.

### 9. Recommendation
- **One clear recommendation** (e.g., "Partner now, buy the data layer, defer build").
- Ranked alternatives table with rationale and confidence.
- **What success looks like** in measurable terms.
- **Guidance:** Unambiguous. If the deck needs to be shared further, this slide must stand alone.

### 10. Roadmap
- Phased plan (e.g., 0–3 / 3–6 / 6–12 months), key milestones, owner, dependency, gate to proceed.
- **Guidance:** Tie each phase to a decision gate and a cost. Executives track money, dates, and owners.

### 11. Commercials (optional — include only if pre-agreed)
- Investment asked, ROI model, pricing summary, payment structure.
- **Guidance:** Omit from a research-only deck unless the client requested commercial framing. If included, align with the proposal in the CRM.

### 12. Next Steps
- **The ask:** the single decision/approval we need from the room, by when.
- 3–5 actions with owners and dates.
- **Guidance:** End with a decision, not a thank-you. Every attendee should know who does what by when.

### 13. Appendix (unlimited)
- Full source list (citation + link + access date).
- Methodology & QA note (mirror `report-template.md` Section 2).
- Detailed tables, model outputs, supporting analysis.
- **Guidance:** Never present appendix slides in-session unless asked; make them printable references.

## Presentation & QA Guidance

- **Timing:** 15–20 minutes for a 30-minute slot (12–18 slides, ~1 min/slide). Leave 10 minutes for discussion.
- **Evidence discipline:** Any claim a reviewer can question must show `[S#]` on-slide or in speaker notes. Uncited slides fail QA.
- **Confidence labeling:** Tag each recommendation with High / Medium / Low confidence — never present estimate as fact.
- **QA gate before delivery:** (1) narrative arc holds start-to-finish, (2) every source is listed in the appendix, (3) the recommendation is singular and actionable, (4) the ask is explicit, (5) classification/version correct.
- **Speaker notes:** Write full notes under each slide — the deck must be presentable by any Meridian principal without the author present.
