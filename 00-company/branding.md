# Brand Guidelines — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Owned by Marketing agent · Living document; refresh with A/B data.

## 1. Brand essence

Meridian = the line of sight that crosses the sky's highest point — the vantage
from which decisions become clear. The brand promises **clarity ahead of
time** ("Decisions beyond the horizon.").

**Archetype:** The Sage (advisory/insight) with the speed of The Magician (AI).
**Personality:** Precise · Confident · Forward-looking · Approachable · Evidence-driven.

## 2. Logo & mark

- **Primary mark:** a stylized meridian arc (a rising horizon line with a
  crossing vertical axis) — minimal, geometric, professional.
- **Wordmark:** "MERIDIAN" in a modern geometric sans, "Research & Advisory"
  as a letter-spaced subline.
- **Monogram:** "M▲" (the triangle = the horizon apex) for favicons and avatars.
- **Clear space:** 1× height of the wordmark on all sides.
- **Do:** single color on white/dark; full-color on print.
- **Don't:** skew, recolor gradients, add shadows, place on busy backgrounds.

## 3. Color palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Meridian Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Primary text, dark sections |
| Horizon Blue | `#1B5FD9` | Primary actions, links, charts |
| Aurora Teal | `#14B8A6` | Secondary accent, highlights, success |
| Signal Amber | `#F59E0B` | Alerts, "attention" markers, CTAs (sparingly) |
| Paper White | `#FAFAF7` | Backgrounds, report pages |
| Slate | `#5B6B7C` | Secondary text, captions |
| Line | `#E3E7EC` | Dividers, borders |

Accessibility: text-on-background contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) in all templates.

## 4. Typography

- **Headlines:** a geometric sans — e.g., `Space Grotesk` (web), fallback `Segoe UI`.
- **Body:** `Inter` (web) / `Georgia` for long-form reports (readability).
- **Data:** `IBM Plex Mono` for figures, tables, and dashboard numbers.
- Sizes: H1 34–48px · H2 24–30px · H3 18–22px · body 15–16px · caption 12–13px.
- Line height 1.5 body; 1.15 headlines.

## 5. Voice & tone

**Say it straight.** Lead with the answer. Support with evidence. No hype.

- Use active voice: "We analyzed 42 vendors" not "An analysis was conducted."
- Numbers with units and dates: "$1.4M by 2028 (CAGR 18%)".
- Name sources inline; link them. Never say "experts say" without a name.
- Admit uncertainty precisely: "40% confidence, based on 3 public sources."
- Disclose AI assistance on every deliverable footer:
  *"Prepared by Meridian Research & Advisory. Analysis is AI-assisted and human-reviewed."*

**Tone rules by channel:**
- LinkedIn: professional, insight-led, 2–4 short paragraphs, a provable claim.
- Email: warm but efficient; subject = concrete benefit; one CTA.
- Reports: formal, structured, source-linked, executive summary first.
- Social threads: teach one specific technique, proof + example.

## 6. Content standards

- Every public claim links a dated source or internal methodology note.
- Every deliverable passes the QA gate (`12-quality/`).
- No fearmongering, no "AI will replace you" doom, no guaranteed ROI promises.
- Case studies anonymize client data unless written consent exists.
