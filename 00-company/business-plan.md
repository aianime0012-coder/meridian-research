# Business Plan — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Prepared by the Strategy & CEO agents · Owner review: required at registration gate.

## 1. Executive summary

Meridian Research & Advisory is an AI-first research and consulting firm that
sells evidence-backed market intelligence and practical AI strategy to
SMBs, scaling startups, and mid-market companies. The entire company is
operated by autonomous AI agents running on Meridian OS, with a single human
shareholder. By treating delivery as software, Meridian undercuts traditional
boutique consultancies on price while exceeding them on speed and continuity.

**Thesis:** professional services are becoming software. The firm that
automates research → insight → recommendation → delivery end-to-end wins on
margin and speed. Meridian is built for that world from day one.

## 2. Problem

- SMBs and mid-market firms face AI disruption but cannot afford $20k–$200k
  strategy engagements from tier-1 consultancies.
- Existing research (Gartner-style) is gated, slow, and general — not tailored
  to a specific company's market, competitor, or timeline.
- Decisions ("build, buy, or partner?") get made on hype instead of evidence.
- Consultancies are slow to adopt AI themselves, so their delivery cost stays high.

## 3. Solution

Productized research + advisory with three delivery modes:

1. **Subscription intelligence** — continuous monitoring of a client's market,
   competitors, and AI-relevant signals, delivered as weekly briefs + live dashboards.
2. **Project research & strategy** — bounded engagements (sprint-shaped) with
   guaranteed deliverables and dates.
3. **Fractional AI advisory** — an always-available AI "chief of staff" for the
   client's leadership, backed by Meridian research.

Every deliverable passes the Meridian QA gate (`12-quality/`) and is source-linked.

## 4. Market

- **Global management consulting**: ~$800B+ (2026, growing ~8–10%/yr).
- **Market research**: ~$90B+ (2026).
- **Target TAM**: North America + EU mid-market organizations adopting AI
  (estimated 1M+ addressable accounts).
- **SOM (Year 3 target)**: 150–300 active accounts across subscriptions,
  retainers, and projects.

## 5. Go-to-market (see `04-marketing/`, `02-leadgen/`, `03-sales/`)

- **Inbound**: SEO content engine + lead magnets (AI Readiness Scorecard,
  Industry AI Snapshots), newsletter, webinars.
- **Outbound**: automated research-driven outreach (LinkedIn + email) to
  accounts with AI-adoption signals; multi-touch sequences.
- **Partner channel**: fractional CTO/consulting networks, industry associations.
- **Product-led**: free insight products funnel users into subscriptions.

## 9. Key risks (top 10 — full register in `10-legal/risk-register.md`)

1. AI output quality inconsistency → mitigated by QA gates + human review of legal/signed docs.
2. Client churn from over-promising → scoping discipline, KPI-linked deliverables.
3. Platform/API dependency (LinkedIn, email) → multi-channel, provider-agnostic design.
4. Legal/regulatory exposure (misrepresentation, GDPR, AI Act) → compliance by design, review gate.
5. Revenue concentration → subscription base + diversification.
6. Brand trust deficit ("AI company") → human owner accountability, transparent methodology.
7. Data privacy incidents → security framework in `01-departments/25-security.md`.
8. Payment/AML issues → Stripe-native KYC/AML, never bypass compliance.
9. Talent/LLM cost volatility → provider-agnostic LLM layer, model-routing.
10. Founder time scarcity → automation-first; owner intervenes only at gates.

## 11. Legal & compliance posture

- All client-facing contracts reviewed at the Legal gate before signature.
- Payments via licensed processors (Stripe/PayPal) with full KYC/AML/OFAC compliance.
- GDPR/AI-Act-ready privacy program; DPAs for EU data.
- Every AI-assisted deliverable discloses methodology; humans accountable for legal output.
- Full posture in `10-legal/`.

## 12. Improvement loop

The company self-audits daily (`13-improvement/`): what failed, why, how to
automate/simplify/delegate. The improvement engine feeds back into workflows,
SOPs, and agent definitions continuously.
