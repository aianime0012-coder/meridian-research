# Service Catalog — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Every service has a delivery SOP (see `11-knowledge/sop-index.md`) and a legal template (`10-legal/`).

## 1. AI Strategy & Readiness Sprint

A fast, bounded engagement that turns "we should use AI" into a prioritized
roadmap with business cases.

**Includes:** AI opportunity scan, readiness assessment (data/process/skills),
3 priority use-cases with ROI estimates, 90-day roadmap, stakeholder brief.
**Format:** 2–4 weeks, synchronous kickoff + async delivery.
**Deliverables:** Readiness Scorecard, Opportunity Map, Roadmap, Executive Brief.
**Price:** $5,000–$15,000 fixed (scoped by company size/data complexity).
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-sprint.md`.

## 2. Competitive & Market Intelligence Subscription (flagship)

Continuous, always-on monitoring of a client's competitive set, market
dynamics, AI-relevant signals, and buyer behavior — delivered weekly.

**Includes:** live intelligence dashboard, weekly brief (source-linked),
quarterly deep-dive, ask-us-anything access to the research desk.
**Format:** rolling monthly subscription.
**Deliverables:** Dashboard, Weekly Brief, Quarterly Deep-Dive, Alert feed.
**Price:** $1,500–$5,000/mo (by # competitors monitored & industries).
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-intelligence-subscription.md`.

## 3. Custom Research Report

Bounded research engagement on a defined question (market entry, vendor
selection, technology assessment, total cost of ownership).

**Includes:** methodology note, primary/secondary research, analysis,
recommendations, appendix with sources.
**Format:** 2–8 weeks by depth (Sprint / Standard / Deep-Dive).
**Deliverables:** Structured report + data appendix + findings briefing.
**Price:** $3,000 (Sprint) · $8,000 (Standard) · $15,000–$30,000 (Deep-Dive).
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-custom-research.md`.

## 4. Fractional AI Advisory Retainer

An always-available AI chief-of-staff for the client's leadership: weekly
strategy sessions, async Q&A, decision memos, vendor shortlists, and board
prep — backed by Meridian's research desk.

**Format:** rolling monthly retainer, 10–40 hours of agent+human-equivalent
delivery per month.
**Deliverables:** Weekly decision memos, on-demand Q&A, meeting prep, board pack.
**Price:** $5,000–$15,000/mo.
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-fractional-advisory.md`.

## 5. Data & Insight Products

Productized assets: industry AI snapshots, dataset subscriptions, market
signal feeds, and white-labeled briefs.

**Examples:** AI Adoption Benchmark (quarterly), Vertical Snapshots
(healthcare/fintech/manufacturing), Signal Feed (API/CSV).
**Format:** subscription or one-time license.
**Price:** $200–$2,000/mo or $500–$5,000 one-time.
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-data-products.md`.

## 6. Training & Enablement

Practical workshops and enablement packs for client teams adopting AI.

**Examples:** "AI literacy for leaders" workshop, "Prompt engineering for analysts",
"AI vendor evaluation playbook".
**Format:** 1–2 day workshop (remote or onsite) + materials pack.
**Price:** $2,000–$8,000 flat.
**SOP:** `05-delivery/sop-training.md`.

Bundles drive MRR: each one-time service includes a discounted path onto a
subscription.

## Scope guardrails

- Every engagement requires a signed SOW or MSA before work begins.
- Deliverable dates are guaranteed only when client inputs arrive on time.
- Research recommendations are advisory, not legal/financial/investment advice.
- Engagement terms in `10-legal/msa.md` + `10-legal/sow-template.md`.
