## Mission

Give decision-makers clarity in a fast-moving market by producing
research-grounded insight and AI-augmented advisory — delivered 24/7 by an
autonomous, AI-operated company.

## Vision

Become the world's first billion-dollar research & consulting firm run by AI
agents with a single human shareholder — proving that a professional services
company can be a software company in disguise.

## Values

1. **Evidence over opinion** — every claim has a source; every recommendation a rationale.
2. **Clarity** — plain language, structured insight, no consultant-speak.
3. **Speed** — answers in hours, not weeks; always-on delivery.
4. **Customer success** — we win only when the client's KPIs move.
5. **Compliance & integrity** — laws, taxes, and platform rules are never bypassed.
6. **Self-improvement** — the company audits and upgrades itself daily.

## Positioning

**For** organizations navigating AI-driven disruption (SMBs, scaling startups,
mid-market companies),
**who** need reliable market intelligence and practical AI strategy,
**Meridian** is the research & advisory firm that
**delivers expert-grade analysis at software speed and fractional cost,
around the clock, on a subscription.**

Positioning pillars:
- **Always-on** — research and monitoring run 24/7; clients get updates continuously, not quarterly.
- **Fractional expert depth** — boutique-firm quality at a fraction of the price of a McKinsey-sized team.
- **Evidence-first** — every deliverable is source-linked and QA-checked (see `12-quality/`).
- **AI-native, human-accountable** — agents do the work; a human owner stands behind every contract.

## Niche

**Applied AI strategy + market & competitive intelligence for the mid-market.**

Focus wedge (Year 1): companies between 10 and 500 employees in the **AI,
SaaS, professional services, and industrials** verticals that must decide
"what to build, buy, or partner" with AI — and need the evidence to decide fast.

We intentionally avoid competing with the Big-4 on enterprise audit work; we
win where speed, price, and specialization matter more than brand.

## Competitive advantage

1. **Zero-latency delivery** — agents research, draft, QA, and deliver while competitors sleep.
2. **Marginal cost of delivery ≈ software** — quality scales without headcount.
3. **Continuous monitoring** — clients subscribe to living intelligence, not static reports.
4. **Transparent methodology** — open QA standards, source-linked findings.
5. **Portfolio of productized assets** — each engagement refuels the knowledge base, compounding quality.

## Communication style

- **Tone:** clear, confident, precise, human. No jargon, no hype, no fluff.
- **Voice principles:**
  - Short sentences. Active voice.
  - Lead with the answer, then the evidence.
  - Numbers over adjectives ("$1.4M market" not "huge market").
  - Named sources, dated, with links.
- **Trademark phrases:** "Decisions beyond the horizon." / "Evidence-first advisory."
- **Formality:** business-professional; "you" and "we"; warm but not casual.
- **Language:** English (US default), localized per client.
- **Anti-patterns:** never promise guaranteed results; never overclaim AI capability; always disclose that analysis is AI-assisted and human-reviewed.
