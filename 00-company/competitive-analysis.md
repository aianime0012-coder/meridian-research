# Competitive Analysis — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Source: agent research of public pricing/marketing. Refresh quarterly via Research agent.

## Competitive landscape

| Competitor | Type | Strengths | Weaknesses | Meridian edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| McKinsey / BCG / Bain | Tier-1 strategy | Brand, talent, enterprise access | $50k–$1M fees, slow, limited AI adoption, not affordable for SMB | 10–100× cheaper, 10× faster, subscription continuity |
| Gartner / Forrester | Analyst firms | Deep databases, brand | Gated, generic, subscription >$20k/yr, no bespoke advisory | Tailored to client's market, price, hands-on advisory |
| Boutique research shops (AlphaSights etc.) | Expert networks | Expert access | Expensive per-call, no continuous intelligence | Continuous monitoring + productized reports |
| Freelance consultants (Upwork) | Individual | Cheap, flexible | Inconsistent quality, no QA, no continuity | QA gate, methodology, team depth, accountability |
| AI tools / "AI consultants" | Commodity | Cheap | No methodology, no verification, hallucination risk | Evidence-first QA, source-linked, human accountability |
| Data/AI agencies | Builders | Execution | Build-first, research shallow | Research-first, decide before you build |

## Why we win

1. **Cost structure:** agents ≈ software. Delivery margin > 85% vs. 40–60% for human firms.
2. **Speed:** continuous monitoring vs. quarterly engagements.
3. **Evidence discipline:** QA gate + source linking beats both freelancers and AI-only tools.
4. **Accountability:** a registered entity with a human shareholder signs contracts and stands behind work — a trust layer pure-AI tools lack.
5. **Compounding knowledge:** every engagement feeds the knowledge base, so the next one is faster and better (flywheel).

## Threat watch (Research agent monitors monthly)

- Tier-1 consultancies launching AI-native SMB products.
- Analyst firms unbundling subscriptions.
- Platform-native "AI agent consultancies" emerging.
- Price compression from automated research tools.
- Regulatory constraints (AI Act) changing liability for AI advisory.

**Response playbook:** stay ahead on vertical specialization, publish methodology openly (trust), and keep cost structure unbeatable.

## Benchmark data (input to pricing)

- Gartner subscription: $20k–$80k/yr.
- Boutique research engagement: $15k–$60k.
- Fractional executive: $3k–$15k/mo.
- Tier-1 AI strategy sprint: $50k–$300k.

Meridian prices at 5–25% of comparable incumbent pricing while delivering more continuity.
