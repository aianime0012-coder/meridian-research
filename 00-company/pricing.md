# Pricing Strategy — Meridian Research & Advisory

> Version 1.0 · Rationale: maximize recurring revenue, price on value, stay undercut on price / over-perform on speed vs tier-1 consultancies.

## Principles

1. **Recurring first** — every engagement is a path to MRR. Subscription attach ≥ 60% of new logos.
2. **Value-based, not hourly** — prices are fixed or monthly, tied to outcome scope, never hourly billing.
3. **Transparent tiers** — clients see price bands and what's included; no hidden change orders.
4. **Land-and-expand** — entry price is deliberately low (low friction), expansion is engineered (upsells, `03-sales/upsell-playbook.md`).
5. **Justifiable by numbers** — a $3k report must return $30k+ of decision value; our sales deck says so.

## Discount policy

- **Annual prepay:** −10% on subscriptions (improves cash flow; invoiced upfront).
- **Founding clients (first 10):** −20% for first 3 months in exchange for a public case study + testimonial.
- **Non-profit / education:** −25% on intelligence subscriptions.
- **Never below cost + 40% gross margin.** Cost floor computed by Finance agent in `08-finance/margin-model.md`.

## Expansion logic (upsell ladder)

1. Starter Intelligence → Intelligence Subscription (attach at report delivery).
2. Intelligence → + Fractional Advisory (observed when client asks "so what do we do?").
3. Advisory → + Readiness Sprint (when client starts building).
4. All → Enterprise Watch (annualized, multi-team seats).
5. Cross-sell Data Products to research-heavy accounts.

## Renewal policy

- Subscriptions auto-renew monthly; annualized where agreed.
- Renewal = re-scope conversation, not invoice only. Client Success agent runs
  `03-sales/renewal-playbook.md` starting day 45.
- Churn triggers a root-cause review within 24h (`13-improvement/`).

## Packaging rules

- One-time services always quote **with** an attach offer; sales automation enforces this in the proposal template.
- Pricing exceptions >10% require CEO agent approval (decision authority in `01-departments/03-sales.md`).
- Prices are reviewed quarterly by the Finance + Strategy agents against competitive benchmark data (`00-company/competitive-analysis.md`).

## Currency & terms

- USD default; local pricing where legal/tax requires (via Stripe multi-currency).
- Net-15 for one-time invoices; subscriptions charged at start of period.
- Late payment: 1.5%/mo, documented in `10-legal/msa.md`.
